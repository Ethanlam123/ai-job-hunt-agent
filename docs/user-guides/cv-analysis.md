# CV Analysis User Guide

This guide walks you through using the AI-powered CV analysis feature to get comprehensive feedback and improvement suggestions for your curriculum vitae.

## Overview

The CV Analysis feature uses advanced AI to:
- Analyze your CV structure and content
- Identify areas for improvement
- Provide specific, actionable suggestions
- Generate improved versions with your approval
- Ensure privacy with human-in-the-loop approval

## Getting Started

### Prerequisites

1. **Valid CV file** in one of these formats:
   - PDF (recommended)
   - DOCX
   - TXT

2. **User account** with login credentials

### Accessing CV Analysis

1. Log into your account
2. Navigate to **Dashboard** → **CV Analysis**
3. Click "Start CV Analysis"

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Upload or Select CV

You have two options:

#### Option A: Upload New CV

1. Click **"Upload New CV"**
2. Choose your CV file (PDF, DOCX, or TXT)
3. Wait for upload completion
4. Preview the parsed content to verify accuracy

#### Option B: Use Existing CV

1. Click **"Select Existing CV"**
2. Choose from your previously uploaded documents
3. Click **"Use This CV"**

### Step 2: Start Analysis

1. Review your selected CV
2. Click **"Start Analysis"**
3. The AI will begin processing your CV

**What happens during analysis:**
- Document parsing and content extraction
- Structure analysis (sections, formatting)
- Content quality assessment
- Comparison with best practices
- Improvement suggestion generation

**Processing time**: Typically 30-60 seconds

### Step 3: Review Analysis Results

Once analysis is complete, you'll see:

#### Overall Assessment
- **Score**: Overall CV quality rating (0-100)
- **Summary**: Brief overview of strengths and areas for improvement
- **Key Metrics**: Word count, section count, readability score

#### Detailed Analysis Sections

##### 1. Structure & Formatting
- **Section Organization**: Logical flow and completeness
- **Formatting**: Consistency and professionalism
- **Length**: Appropriate length for your experience level

##### 2. Content Quality
- **Experience Descriptions**: Impact and clarity
- **Skills Section**: Relevance and organization
- **Education**: Completeness and presentation
- **Achievements**: Quantifiable results and impact

##### 3. Language & Tone
- **Professional Language**: Appropriate terminology
- **Active Voice**: Action-oriented descriptions
- **Clarity**: Readability and comprehension

##### 4. Industry Best Practices
- **ATS Optimization**: Keyword optimization
- **Modern Standards**: Current CV conventions
- **Industry-Specific**: Tailored to your field

### Step 4: Review Improvement Suggestions

Each suggestion includes:

- **Category**: What aspect it addresses (structure, content, formatting, etc.)
- **Priority**: High, Medium, or Low importance
- **Description**: What to change and why
- **Example**: Before/after comparison
- **Impact**: How this change improves your CV

#### Example Suggestion:

**Category**: Content Quality
**Priority**: High
**Description**: Add quantifiable achievements to your experience section
**Current**: "Managed a team of developers"
**Suggested**: "Managed a team of 8 developers, increasing productivity by 25%"
**Impact**: Demonstrates measurable impact and leadership effectiveness

### Step 5: Approve or Reject Suggestions

For each suggestion, you can:

1. **Review the details**: Click on any suggestion to see full explanation
2. **Approve**: Click ✓ to accept the suggestion
3. **Reject**: Click ✗ to decline the suggestion
4. **Modify**: Add your own changes in the notes field

**Bulk Actions**:
- **Approve All**: Accept all high and medium priority suggestions
- **Reject All**: Decline all suggestions
- **Custom Selection**: Choose specific suggestions to apply

### Step 6: Generate Improved CV

1. After making your selections, click **"Apply Improvements"**
2. Review the summary of changes
3. Click **"Generate Improved CV"**
4. Wait for AI to apply approved suggestions

### Step 7: Download and Review

1. **Preview**: Review the improved CV side-by-side with original
2. **Download**: Save the improved version as PDF
3. **Compare**: Use the comparison view to see all changes
4. **Iterate**: Start a new analysis if needed

## Understanding the Analysis

### Scoring System

- **90-100**: Excellent - Professional, well-structured CV
- **80-89**: Good - Minor improvements needed
- **70-79**: Fair - Several areas for improvement
- **60-69**: Needs Work - Significant improvements required
- **Below 60**: Poor - Major restructuring needed

### Common Improvement Areas

#### High Priority Issues
- Missing contact information
- Poor section organization
- Vague experience descriptions
- Lack of quantifiable achievements
- Inconsistent formatting

#### Medium Priority Issues
- Weak action verbs
- Missing skills section
- Outdated design elements
- Incomplete education details

#### Low Priority Issues
- Minor formatting inconsistencies
- Word choice optimization
- Section ordering tweaks
- Design enhancements

## Best Practices

### Before Analysis
1. **Use a clean, recent version** of your CV
2. **Ensure file is readable** and not password-protected
3. **Check file size** (under 10MB recommended)
4. **Verify content accuracy** before upload

### During Analysis
1. **Take your time** reviewing each suggestion
2. **Consider your target industry** when evaluating suggestions
3. **Think about your career goals** and which improvements align
4. **Don't feel pressured** to accept every suggestion

### After Analysis
1. **Proofread the improved CV** carefully
2. **Test readability** by sharing with a trusted colleague
3. **Keep both versions** for comparison
4. **Update other job search materials** (LinkedIn, etc.)

## Tips for Maximum Value

### 1. Be Selective with Suggestions
- Focus on high-priority items first
- Consider your specific industry requirements
- Maintain your authentic voice and style

### 2. Understand the "Why"
- Read explanations for each suggestion
- Learn the reasoning behind recommendations
- Apply principles to future CV updates

### 3. Iterate and Improve
- Use the analysis multiple times as you gain experience
- Update your CV after each job application cycle
- Track which improvements generate better responses

### 4. Complement with Human Feedback
- Share improved CV with mentors or colleagues
- Get feedback from industry professionals
- Use AI suggestions as a starting point, not final word

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### Upload Problems
- **Issue**: File won't upload
- **Solution**: Check file format (PDF, DOCX, TXT only), ensure file size < 10MB

#### Parsing Errors
- **Issue**: Content appears garbled or incomplete
- **Solution**: Try re-saving PDF as text file, check document quality

#### Analysis Taking Too Long
- **Issue**: Analysis seems stuck
- **Solution**: Refresh page, check internet connection, try again

#### Suggestions Not Relevant
- **Issue**: AI suggestions don't match your industry
- **Solution**: Use the "Reject" option and focus on relevant improvements

## FAQ

**Q: How accurate is the CV analysis?**
A: The analysis uses advanced AI trained on thousands of professional CVs and industry best practices. Accuracy is typically 85-95% for structure and content recommendations.

**Q: Will my CV data be shared with third parties?**
A: No. Your CV data is processed securely and is only used to provide analysis. We never share your personal information with third parties.

**Q: Can I analyze multiple CV versions?**
A: Yes. You can upload and analyze different CV versions for different job applications or industries.

**Q: How often should I analyze my CV?**
A: We recommend analyzing your CV every 3-6 months, or whenever you make significant changes to your experience or skills.

**Q: What if I disagree with a suggestion?**
A: That's perfectly fine! The AI provides suggestions based on best practices, but you know your career best. Feel free to reject any suggestions that don't align with your goals.

**Q: Can I save multiple analysis results?**
A: Yes. All your analysis sessions are saved in your history for future reference.

**Q: Is the improvement generation instant?**
A: No. The AI needs time to apply your approved suggestions thoughtfully. This typically takes 30-60 seconds.

**Q: Can I edit the improved CV manually?**
A: Yes. After downloading the improved CV, you can make any additional edits using your preferred document editor.

## Privacy and Security

Your privacy is our priority:

- **Data Encryption**: All files are encrypted in transit and at rest
- **Secure Processing**: CVs are processed in isolated environments
- **No Data Sharing**: We never share your CV with third parties
- **Retention Policy**: You can delete your data at any time
- **Human-in-the-Loop**: All changes require your explicit approval
